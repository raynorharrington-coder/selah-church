Selah Kids.

The little children mattered to Jesus, and they matter to us.

Sundays · During the service

Your kids are a priority here.

Your children are a priority at our church because we believe they are the future of the Church. We are committed to providing intentional care, uncompromised safety, and biblical training that helps children grow in the way of Jesus.

Our goal is to create an environment where kids can thrive spiritually while also having fun in a loving and engaging atmosphere.

At Selah, we partner with LifeWay’s Explore the Bible children’s curriculum, which helps children learn Scripture in a clear, engaging, and age-appropriate way.

What a Sunday looks like

Here’s what your kiddos can expect every week.

1

Check in

Stop by the Selah Kids check-in desk first. We’ll get your child signed in and walk you to their room.

2

Worship

Kids worship along with the rest of the church family, in a space built just for them.

3

Bible stories

Scripture taught clearly, from the Explore the Bible curriculum.

4

Prayer

Space to pray together and learn how to talk to God.

5

Games and fun

Because church should be somewhere kids actually want to be.
