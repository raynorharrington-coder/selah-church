2022 — Today

The spirit of that living room.

Selah began in 2022 as a small house church, just a group of people gathering in a living room to worship Jesus, open the Scriptures, and share life together.

While God has continued to grow our church, our heart has never changed. We still desire to be a spiritual family that worships with sincerity, pursues the presence of God, and walks closely with one another.

No matter how the Lord grows Selah, we always want to keep the spirit of that living room: simple, authentic, and centered on Jesus.
