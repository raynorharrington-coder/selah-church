Selah Youth

Selah Youth exists to guide young people on their spiritual journey, helping them to deepen their relationship with God while fostering a sense of belonging and purpose.

We believe faith is not just something we practice on Sundays but is part of our daily lives. Through worship, teaching, fellowship, and fun we aim to inspire youth to live out their faith in meaningful ways.

All youth group events are held at the church.
