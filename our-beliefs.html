What We Believe

Simple, historic, and central to everything we do.

Fourteen convictions that shape how we gather, teach, and follow Jesus together.

01

The Bible

Scripture is God’s written Word, divinely inspired by the Holy Spirit, without error in original manuscripts. The Bible authoritatively reveals God’s truth regarding faith, life, and practice.

02

Jesus Christ

We affirm that Jesus Christ is Lord and the exclusive path to relationship with God. His sinless life and sacrificial death provide atonement for all who trust in Him alone for salvation.

03

Trinity

We affirm one God existing eternally in three distinct persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

04

Original Sin

Humanity was created in God’s image but fell through disobedience, resulting in physical and spiritual death. All people are born with sinful nature, separated from God’s life, and require Christ’s atoning work for salvation.

05

Resurrection

Jesus Christ physically rose from the dead on the third day, defeating sin and death. His resurrection is the foundation of our hope and guarantees the future resurrection of all who belong to Him.

06

Salvation

Salvation is by grace alone through faith alone in Jesus Christ alone. Through His death and resurrection, all who repent and believe are forgiven, reconciled to God, and receive eternal life.

07

The Church

The Church is the body of Christ, made up of all who have placed their faith in Jesus. God calls believers to gather regularly for worship, discipleship, fellowship, and mission.

08

Holy Spirit / Spirit-Filled Believer

The Holy Spirit continues to empower believers today through spiritual gifts, miracles, healing, and the work of His presence to build up the Church and advance God’s Kingdom.

09

Kingdom Empowerment

God remains active today, working powerfully through believers as vessels for His kingdom purposes.

10

Water Baptism

Water baptism is the public declaration of faith and obedience to Christ for all who have repented and believed.

11

The Lord’s Supper

The Lord’s Supper is a regular remembrance of Christ’s sacrifice, celebrated by believers as an act of worship, gratitude, and proclamation until He returns.

12

Marriage

We believe marriage is a covenant established by God between one man and one woman, reflecting Christ’s covenant love for His Church.

13

Gender & Identity

God created humanity in His image as male and female. We believe our identity is ultimately found in Christ and that God’s design for sex and gender is good, intentional, worthy of joyful obedience, and His design is not ours to redefine or change.

14

The Second Coming

Jesus will return bodily and visibly to judge all humanity and gather His people.

Selah: pause, reflect, praise.
