A small group with a shared mission.

The term Cadre refers to a small group that derives from a larger organization to train, equip, and strengthen one another for a shared mission. We believe this perfectly captures the heart of our small groups.

Cadres are designed to help people build authentic relationships, grow in their faith, and walk through life together. Each gathering creates space to open God’s Word, reflect on Sunday’s sermon, pray for one another, and encourage one another as we follow Jesus side by side.

You don’t have to have it all together to join a Cadre; you just have to be willing to show up, be known, and grow. Whether you’re new to faith or have been following Jesus for years, there’s a place for you.
