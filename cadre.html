Cadre.

Men’s and women’s groups built to train, equip, and call the best out of each other.

Tuesdays · 6:30–8:30 PM

A small group from a larger one.

The term Cadre comes from the French origin as a military word meaning a small group which derives from a larger organization to train and equip one another for the purpose of what they are called to.

This perfectly describes our men’s and women’s cadres. A small group from our church that creates intentional space to worship together, check in on a deeper level, and call the best out of each other as God leads.
