Privacy

How we handle what you share.

Especially prayer requests. If you tell us something, here is exactly where it goes.

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Selah Church runs this website so people can find us, get in touch, and ask for prayer. This page explains what the site collects, who can see it, and how to have it removed.

What this site collects

Only what you type into one of our two forms.

The contact form asks for your name, email address, and message.

The prayer request form asks for your name and your request. Your email address is optional — you can leave it blank and still submit. There is also a checkbox to mark a request confidential.

Nothing else on this site asks you for anything. There is no account to create, no newsletter signup, and no online giving form that collects your details on this website.

How prayer requests are handled

This is the part worth being specific about.

When you submit a prayer request, it is emailed to the church inbox and saved to a private Google Sheet that the church uses to keep track of requests so they are not lost. Only church leadership can open that sheet.

If you tick “keep this request confidential,” the request is marked confidential in that email and in that sheet, and it is shared only with the pastoral team — not read aloud, not included in a prayer list, and not passed to volunteers.

If you would rather nothing be written down at all, please speak to someone in person on a Sunday, or call the church, instead of using this form.

How we use what you send

To respond to you, and to pray for what you have asked us to pray for. That is all.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone outside the church. We will not add you to a mailing list because you contacted us or submitted a prayer request.

Who else sees it

Submissions travel through Google — a Google Apps Script handles the form, Gmail carries the notification, and Google Sheets stores the record. Google processes and stores that data as part of providing those services, under its own privacy policy.

This website is hosted on Cloudflare. As with any web host, Cloudflare processes standard request information — including your IP address — in order to deliver these pages to you. Cloudflare also runs the spam check on our forms, described below.

No one else. We do not use a third-party form service, a mailing platform, or a customer database.

Spam protection on our forms

The contact and prayer forms are protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that a submission comes from a real person rather than an automated script. To do that, your browser contacts Cloudflare, which receives your IP address and general information about your browser. Cloudflare processes this under its own privacy policy. It is used only to tell people apart from bots — not to profile you, and not to advertise to you. It does not see the contents of your message or prayer request until you submit the form.

It loads only on the two pages that have a form. Reading any other page on this site never contacts Cloudflare's spam check.

Cookies, analytics, and tracking

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and carries no advertising or tracking pixels of any kind. We do not know who visits, only that the pages were served.

Maps and embedded content

The map on our contact and visit pages does not load until you click it. Until you do, Google receives nothing from your visit. Once you click to load the map, Google Maps loads in the page and receives your IP address, as it would on any site with a map. That was a deliberate choice so that simply reading the page does not share your visit with Google.

Fonts and other assets

The fonts, images, and scripts that make up this site are served from this website's own domain. Nothing is loaded from Google Fonts or an outside content network that could log your visit.

How long we keep it

Contact messages and prayer requests are kept while they are being responded to and prayed over, and as part of the church's ordinary records afterwards. If you want something removed sooner, just ask — see below.

Your choices

You can read this entire website without submitting anything.

If you have sent us a message or a prayer request and would like it deleted from our inbox and our records, email info@selahchurchfxbg.com and we will remove it. You do not have to explain why.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them through these forms. Information about children involved in Selah Kids is collected in person, not on this website.

Changes to this policy

If what this site collects or how it is handled changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change with it.
