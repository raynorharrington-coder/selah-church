Who We Are

A family, not a crowd.

Selah Church is a non-denominational community in Fredericksburg, VA built around one idea — the church was made for family. We're not interested in putting on a show. We want to know your name, walk with you through the hard seasons, and celebrate the good ones alongside you.

Whether you grew up in church or have never set foot in one, you're welcome exactly as you are. No performance, no pretending — just an honest place to pause, reflect, and praise.
