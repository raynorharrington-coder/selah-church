Who We Are

A family, not a crowd.

Selah Church is a non-denominational community in Fredericksburg, VA built around one idea — the church was made for family. We're not interested in putting on a show. We want to know your name, walk with you through the hard seasons, and celebrate the good ones alongside you.

Whether you grew up in church or have never set foot in one, you're welcome exactly as you are. No performance, no pretending — just an honest place to pause, reflect, and praise.

What we believe

Simple, historic, and central to everything we do.

01

Jesus Christ

We affirm that Jesus Christ is Lord and the exclusive path to relationship with God. His sinless life and sacrificial death provide atonement for all who trust in Him alone for salvation.

02

Trinity

We affirm one God existing eternally in three distinct persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

03

Original Sin

Humanity was created in God's image but fell through disobedience, resulting in physical and spiritual death. All people are born with sinful nature, separated from God's life, and require Christ's atoning work for salvation.

04

Salvation

God's love extends to all people, and salvation is available to everyone through faith in Christ.

05

Gender & Identity

All people bear God's image. Humanity was created male or female, reflecting God's design that cannot be altered.

06

The Bible

Scripture is God's written Word, divinely inspired by the Holy Spirit, without error in original manuscripts. The Bible authoritatively reveals God's truth regarding faith, life, and practice.

07

The Church

Every Spirit-born believer constitutes part of the church body, unified spiritually in Jesus Christ with all other believers.

08

Community

The weekly gathering provides a welcoming space for people to experience rest and encouragement through shared meals, worship, and teaching.

09

Spirit-Filled Believer

God desires believers to be filled with the Holy Spirit and wholly sanctified, separated from sin and dedicated to God's will for holy living and effective service.

10

Kingdom Empowerment

God remains active today, working powerfully through believers as vessels for His kingdom purposes.

11

The Second Coming

Jesus will return bodily and visibly to judge all humanity and gather His people.

12

Water Baptism

Water baptism shall be administered to those who have repented and believed in Christ for salvation.

13

The Lord's Supper

The Lord's Supper ordinance shall be regularly observed by established churches as Scripture directs.

Doctrinal statement sourced from Selah's own site (selahchurchfxbg.com/ourbeliefs) as of 2026-07-31 — worth a quick confirm with Luke's team before launch in case anything's changed since.

Meet the team

The people leading and serving alongside you at Selah.

Photo: Luke

Luke

Lead Pastor

Luke and Amanda have been married since 2020 and are the proud parents of Maverick Isaiah, with their daughter, Haven Joy, on the way. Together, they've served in full-time ministry for over a decade. Before planting Selah, Luke served as a campus pastor at the University of Mary Washington for several years, and holds a Master of Divinity with a concentration in Spiritual Formation from Regent University. In 2022, Luke and Amanda planted Selah Church as a simple house church. At the heart of their ministry is a deep desire to follow the leadership of the Holy Spirit, faithfully teach God's Word, and create a place where people can encounter Jesus.
