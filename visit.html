What to expect

First time coming? Here's how a Sunday morning at Selah flows.

1

Arrive & get settled

Come whenever works — someone will greet you at the door and help you find your way.

2

Drop off the kids

If you're bringing kids, our Selah Kids team will check them in to a safe, age-appropriate room.

3

Worship & the message

Expect a relaxed, come-as-you-are atmosphere — casual dress, worship that isn't rushed through a set list, and a practical message from scripture. Prayer is often offered right in the room as people need it, and our team is always glad to pray with you anytime, before or after.

4

Stick around

Grab a coffee and stay after — this is where the "family" part actually happens.

Still have questions?
